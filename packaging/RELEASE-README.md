# DKR-R v1.0.16

On Windows, extract the full ZIP and open **DKR-R.exe**. On Linux and Steam
Deck, make the AppImage executable and launch it. Keep the Windows launcher,
libexec, assets and licence notices together.

Add your own legally obtained US v1.0 or US Rev A/v1.1 ROM in Play. Some legacy
mods still need US v1.0. Keep your existing profile and backups. For a portable
install, keep portable.txt and dkr-runtime-data with the application.

Open **Speedrunning** for runs, replays and leaderboards. Use matching builds
for multiplayer. **Save Manager** edits separate Vanilla and Modded saves.
Sign in with Discord to enable **Cloud Saves** beside the active save.

See RELEASE-NOTES.txt and the repository guides for requirements, compatibility,
recovery and validation.
