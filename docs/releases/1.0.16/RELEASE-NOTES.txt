DKR-R v1.0.16
Changes since v1.0.15

Speedrunning
- Open Speedrunning from Play. Top Players shows points, published personal
  bests and world records. Open a player to view their results. Track cards
  show leaders by vehicle for Full Race and Best Lap. View Times includes a
  timeline with each record holder and how long their time stood.
- Use Runs > Run to start a Time Trial or Challenge Run. Practice and ranked
  attempts share Start Run. Race Ghost records an attempt with the ghost's
  racer. Visual texture packs stay enabled.
- Sign in with Discord to submit. Eligible local runs can be linked once to an
  account. My Submitted Runs shows review status and the next slot to reopen
  in the rolling 24-hour window.
- Replays offers scrubbing, frame steps, playback speed, recorded inputs and
  prepared ghosts. Playback keeps the recorded racer. Manage local recordings
  and overlay preferences from the launcher.
- Challenge Runs include Any%, 100%, Glitchless, All Bosses, All Minigames,
  All Trophy Races and its ordered variants, Car%, Hover%, Plane%, and
  T.T. Amulet without wrong warps. Automatic splits follow route milestones.
  Check the launcher and board for enabled submission routes.
- Moderators and admins review verified runs. Hosted videos show player
  credit, controller inputs and Challenge Run splits. Rendering can continue
  after upload. The portal bell reports submission and review updates.

Online
- Browse public lobbies and recorded player activity on the portal. Use
  connection measurements to compare hosts. A host can offer ownership to
  another member without closing the lobby. Seats and save ownership persist.
- Racers send inputs directly when available, with the host path available
  for recovery. A lobby supports four racers and eight spectators. Spectators
  buffer confirmed frames independently.
- Rollback presentation retains pictures through brief corrections, and
  automatic delay accounts for forward progress and replay cost.
- Upstream improves relay and shutdown behavior, controller binding recovery,
  custom boss races and checkpoint capacity. Use matching builds online.

Cloud Saves and the launcher
- Sign in with Discord and enable Cloud Saves beside the active save on Play.
  The cloud icon reports sync status and runs a manual sync when selected.
- Vanilla, Modded and Online Adventure sync independently. Session Mods
  chooses the Modded Adventure. Existing personal progress stays in Vanilla.
  Development saves are separate.
- Cloud Saves refresh around launching, hosting and returning to the launcher.
  Successful game writes show Saving Progress and queue a sync. If copies
  diverge, the cloud copy takes priority and local recovery keeps the old copy.
- Save Manager puts the slots beside the editor. Progress shows all editing
  tools and Max Out actions. Files groups transfer and recovery.
- Badge Gallery adds search, filters, badge details and pinning. The launcher
  and overlay use padded dialogs, compact Play settings and Speedrunning
  loading transitions.

Graphics and modding
- Replay widescreen HUD uses fit-to-window. Website videos capture up to
  1080p at 60 fps where the category permits, with a host render profile.
- Contributor work includes water caustics and vehicle spray, road wheels,
  Track Lab challenge/boss/hub support, SDK schema documentation, generated
  symbols and build tooling. The rollback self-test also accepts a Track Lab
  course and reports the number of checkpoints it loaded.

Extract the complete Windows ZIP and use DKR-R.exe. On Linux or Steam Deck
make the AppImage executable. Keep your profile and supply your own US v1.0
or US Rev A/v1.1 ROM. Some legacy mod patches require US v1.0. Desktop targets
are Windows x64, Linux x86_64 and Linux ARM64. Steam Deck uses Linux x86_64.
Ranked uploads require an eligible x86_64 build.
