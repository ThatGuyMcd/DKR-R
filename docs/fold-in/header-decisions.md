# Header decisions (fold step B1)

The v1.0.11 binaries were compiled from staged copies, and 13 headers reached
different TUs in more than one version. A single-copy tree keeps one version
of each. This page says which one and why; `scripts/fold/header-variants.json`
is the machine-readable form (chosen sha256 plus every TU that compiled
against another version), regenerated by
`scripts/fold/apply_closure.py --write-variants` and checked by `--check`.

The rule (fold plan 2.3): keep the version the defining TU compiled against,
and the upstream-ported version where PRs 66 and 68 require it. The other
TUs are allowed to differ at equivalence level 2 (preprocessed tokens) and
must instead match at level 3 (object code), which shows the declarations
that changed are unused in them.

## Forwarders (7, plus one of the three `magic_code_package.hpp` copies)

`mod-runtime-repair` staged a two-line copy of each of these headers,
`#pragma once` and an absolute `#include` of the real SDK2 header. Collapsing
a forwarder into its target leaves every TU's tokens unchanged, so these are
not real splits. Plan 3.1 drops the forwarders.

| Header | Kept | TUs that went through the forwarder |
|---|---|---|
| `mods/magic_code_game_services.hpp` | SDK2_BASE | `magic_code_game_world.cpp` |
| `mods/magic_code_game_world.hpp` | SDK2 | `magic_code_game_world.cpp` |
| `mods/magic_code_link.hpp` | SDK2 | `magic_code_link.cpp` |
| `mods/magic_code_native_arguments.hpp` | SDK2_BASE | `magic_code_game_world.cpp` |
| `mods/magic_code_vm.hpp` | SDK2 | the five `mods/magic_code_*.cpp` TUs |
| `mods/magic_code_world.hpp` | SDK2 | `magic_code_world.cpp` |
| `mods/sdk2_schema.hpp` | SDK2_BASE | `magic_code_package.cpp` |
| `mods/magic_code_package.hpp` (forwarder copy) | SDK2 | `magic_code_package.cpp` |

## Real splits (6)

| Header | Kept | Minority TUs | What differs for them |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mobile_graphics_preset.hpp` | PR 68 (upstream-sync, same as main) | `rt64_renderer.cpp` | gains `<optional>`, `preset_anisotropy()` and `matching_preset()`; it calls neither |
| `mods/legacy_character_materialize.hpp` | SDK2 (44 TUs, incl. `legacy_character_materialize.cpp`) | `vehicle_binding.cpp`, `vehicle_resources.cpp` | gains `PreparedCharacter::source_portrait{,_id}` and a `prepare_character()` parameter; neither TU names either |
| `mods/magic_code_dispatch.h` | PR 66 (upstream-sync, same as main) | 154 TUs (1 first-party, the rest guest and prepared payloads) | gains an `#if defined(__APPLE__)` `__thread` block; identical tokens off Apple |
| `mods/magic_code_package.hpp` | SDK2 (38 TUs) | `vehicle_library.cpp`, `vehicle_package.cpp` | `code::Package` gains the `sdk2_*`, `development` and `debug_lines` members; both TUs use the vehicle `Package`, not this one |
| `resident_music.hpp` | music-preview-repair (incl. `resident_music.cpp`) | `mods/online_mod_runtime.cpp` | gains the `PreviewScope` class; unused there |
| `save_manager.hpp` | SHARED_SAVES (incl. `save_manager.cpp`) | `runtime_save_routing.cpp` | gains `StoredSaveKind::CustomRecords`, `content_hash`/`copies` and `stored_adventures(limit, truncated)`; it names none of them |

The minority TUs of the real splits are the level 3 set. If one of them does
not match at the object level, the shipped binary used the differing
declaration, and that TU gets its own decision here before the fold merges.
